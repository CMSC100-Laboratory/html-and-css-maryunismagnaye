AUTOBIOGRAPHY

As a soon-to-be CS grad, I’ve spent years in the trenches of frontend development, battling CSS specificity wars, debugging null pointer exceptions in JavaScript like a try-catch wizard, and optimizing render times with enough Big-O obsession to make my professors weep. My Git commit history reads like a saga of “final_final_real_fix_v2,” and I’ve mastered the art of turning wireframes into stateful, componentized, API-powered magic. I dream in flexboxes and grid layouts, and my definition of a smooth user experience involves fewer than three console errors. Ready to merge into the Gugil Co. main branch, I promise to ship features faster than a junior dev can Google “how to center a div.”
